FAQ

Common Questions

Quick answers before you reach out.

Is TechLearn really free? +
Yes — all core course content is completely free. No credit card, no hidden fees.
Do I need a computer to learn? +
For most courses, yes — a computer is recommended, especially for coding. Some theory content can be accessed on a phone.
Are the certificates recognised by employers? +
Our certificates show proof of completion and the skills you've built. Many employers value practical portfolio projects — which you'll build in every course.
Can I suggest a course topic? +
Absolutely! Use the contact form above and select "General Enquiry". We actively build new content based on learner demand.
How do I report a bug or broken content? +
Use the form above and select "Report an Issue". Please include the course name and what you were trying to do.
