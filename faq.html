General

About TechLearn

What is TechLearn? +
TechLearn is a free online platform that offers structured tech courses for beginners and intermediate learners. We cover web development, Python, data science, cybersecurity, and more.
Is TechLearn really free? +
Yes — all core course content is completely free. No credit card, no hidden fees. We may offer optional premium features in future, but learning will always be free.
Who is TechLearn for? +
TechLearn is for anyone who wants to learn tech skills — whether you're a complete beginner, a student, or a professional looking to upskill. Our courses are designed to be accessible to everyone.
Do I need prior experience to start? +
Not at all! Most of our courses start from absolute zero. We have beginner tracks specifically designed for people with no coding experience whatsoever.
Courses

Learning on TechLearn

How long does each course take? +
It depends on the course. Our shortest courses are around 8 hours and the longest are 32+ hours. You can go at your own pace — there are no deadlines.
Can I access courses on my phone? +
Yes! TechLearn is mobile-friendly. However for coding courses, we recommend using a laptop or desktop so you can practice writing code alongside the lessons.
Do I get a certificate when I finish? +
Yes — you earn a certificate of completion for every course you finish. You can download and share it on LinkedIn or add it to your CV.
Can I suggest a new course topic? +
Absolutely! We love hearing from our community. Use the Contact page to send us your suggestion and we'll consider it for our next content sprint.

Still have questions?

We're happy to help. Reach out and we'll get back to you within 24 hours.
